What we stand for

Built on three promises

Honest materials

Kiln-dried teak, walnut and sheesham — never veneer pretending to be wood. Every board is inspected before it earns the Aangan mark.

Real craftsmanship

Mortise-and-tenon joints, hand-rubbed finishes and frames built to carry a lifetime of iftars, weddings and quiet evenings.

Fair to everyone

Fair wages for our artisans, fair prices for our customers, and a 5-year warranty that says we stand behind every piece.
